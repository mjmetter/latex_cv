\addblocktext{Summary}{%
Engineering leader with 15 years of professional experience, including 11 years scaling platform and infrastructure organizations at Bloomreach -- from a single \textasciitilde{}9-person hosting-platform team to a 45+ engineer, three-business-unit organization spanning the Netherlands, India, the US, and Czech Republic/Slovakia. Track record of turning an unmeasured, sub-50\%-margin hosting platform into a 95\%+ margin product by building cost visibility where none existed and negotiating investment directly with finance; consolidating two competing infrastructure generations into one, cloud-migrated and auto-scaled platform; and establishing infra/application ownership boundaries and unified tooling across a 50+ engineer product organization with no prior platform structure. Most recently led company-wide AI coding-tool adoption from pilot to enterprise scale across 255 engineers. Running three structurally distinct products at once surfaced a clear limit to that model: sustaining deep, trust-based platform leadership requires enough focus per product to remain credible in each -- a lesson now shaping a deliberate preference for scope built on one coherent core rather than several unrelated ones.
}
